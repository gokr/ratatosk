# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 12, 2026

**App Name:** Ratatosk

Welcome to Ratatosk (the "App"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our application.

## 1. Information We Collect

When you choose to sign in to our App using Google Sign-In, we access limited information provided by your Google account to authenticate your identity and set up your account. Specifically, we request and access:

* **OpenID (`openid`):** Used to verify your unique user identifier securely.

* **Email Address (`https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email`):** Used to identify your account and communicate with you regarding essential app services.

* **Profile Information (`https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.profile`):** Typically includes your name and profile picture, which we use to personalize your experience within the App.

We do not collect any additional personal data beyond what is provided through this authentication process.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the data collected strictly for the following purposes:

* To create and manage your user account.

* To authenticate your identity when you log in.

* To display your basic profile information (such as your name and avatar) within the App interface.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

## 3. Data Storage and Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Your data is stored securely and only for as long as your account remains active.

## 4. Third-Party Services

Our App uses Google Sign-In for authentication. Your interaction with Google's authentication service is governed by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 5. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You can revoke our App's access to your Google account at any time via your [Google Account Security Settings](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

If you wish to delete your account and all associated data from our systems, you can do so by contacting us at [ratatosk@flatout.works](mailto:ratatosk@flatout.works) or using the in-app account deletion options.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
